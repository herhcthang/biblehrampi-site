Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version. Bible Hrampi is a teaching website. You can read everything on it without an account. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

Who we are

Bible Hrampi is run by Herh Cung Thang, an individual ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your information, email biblehrampi@gmail.com.

This policy covers the website at biblehrampi.com and the Bible Hrampi Vault app. PUMKHAT, our church community app, has its own PUMKHAT Privacy Policy.

The website

What we collect

  • Nothing you type in. The website has no accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes.
  • Basic server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub) records standard request logs, such as your IP address, the page you asked for, the time and your browser type. These keep the site working and secure, and are kept and deleted under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • One preference on your device. If you press "Turn off animation" (or turn it back on), your browser remembers that choice in its own local storage, so the site looks the way you chose next time. It never leaves your device. The site sets no cookies.

What we don't use

  • No advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics.
  • No embedded social media buttons or trackers.
  • Fonts and all 3D artwork are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't contact other companies.

Emailing us

If you email us (for example a prayer request or a question about Scripture), we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We use your message only to reply and to pray for you, and we don't share prayer requests with anyone unless you ask us to. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

The Bible Hrampi Vault app

The Vault app is not available yet. It is being built to be a private vault for your own studies, verses and notes: private by default, with no social features, and with AI used only to organize and retrieve your own words.

Before the Vault app is released, we will publish its full privacy details here: what it stores, where it is stored, which service providers it uses, how its AI features work, and how to delete your data. The app will not collect anything until those details are published.

Children

The website is for a general audience and collects no personal information from visitors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a child under 13 has emailed us, a parent can ask us to delete that email.

International visitors

Bible Hrampi is run from the United States, and our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. If you visit from another country (for example Myanmar, India or elsewhere), basic server logs are processed outside your country, where privacy laws may differ.

Your rights and choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by email.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, or to object to certain uses. Email us and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Vault app is released. We'll change the date at the top, and important changes will be noted on this page.

Contact us

Bible Hrampi is run by Herh Cung Thang. We are reachable by email only: biblehrampi@gmail.com
