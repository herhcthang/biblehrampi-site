5. Community rules

When you use PUMKHAT, please don't:

  • post sexual content, or anything that sexualizes or endangers a minor in any way;
  • contact teens in a way that goes around your church's safety rules;

Prayer requests and church conversations can be personal. Respect people's privacy and don't share what others posted outside the group it was meant for.
